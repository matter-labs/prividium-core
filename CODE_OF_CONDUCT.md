# Code of Conduct

Version: 1.0

Adapted from the Matter Labs Code of Conduct v1.1, which derives from the Apache Software Foundation Code of Conduct
(Apache License 2.0) and from the Mozilla Community Participation Guidelines (CC BY-SA 3.0).

Our goal is a safe, professional, and inclusive space for everyone taking part in the Prividium Core project.

## Where this applies

This Code of Conduct applies to the Prividium Core repository and every space the maintainers run for it: issues, pull
requests, discussions, and project communication channels. It applies at project events, in person and online, and when
someone represents the project in public. It complements rather than replaces legal rights and obligations.

## Expected behaviour

**Be ethical.** Do not infringe on the rights or wellbeing of others, and do not use project spaces to break the law.

**Be kind and respectful.** Treat everyone with empathy and respect. Participants come from different backgrounds,
jurisdictions, and levels of experience.

**Share and learn.** Contribute to discussions, offer helpful feedback, explain your work, and stay open to learning
from others.

**Give credit.** When you contribute content or ideas that are not your own, credit the original author. Plagiarism and
intellectual property infringement are prohibited.

**Respect privacy and confidentiality.** Ask before sharing personal information about yourself or others. Do not post
material that is confidential to your employer, your customers, or a third party.

**Be open to review and improvement.** Code and design decisions are discussed in the open. Stay open to constructive
criticism. Questions are encouraged, and we redirect them to the appropriate channel if we do not have the answer.

**Mind your language.** English is the project's working language. Many participants are not native speakers, so be
succinct and avoid unnecessary acronyms. We aim for an environment welcoming to users of all ages, so avoid profanity
and explicit content.

**Stay on topic.** Keep discussion relevant to Prividium Core. Commercial solicitation, recruitment, and promotion of
unrelated products do not belong in project spaces.

## Unacceptable behaviour

We have a zero-tolerance policy for hate speech, bullying, harassment, and discrimination. This includes, but is not
limited to:

- Violent threats or language directed against another person.
- Sexist, racist, or otherwise discriminatory jokes and language.
- Posting sexually explicit or violent material.
- Posting, or threatening to post, another person's personally identifying information.
- Sharing private content without explicit consent, including messages sent privately or non-publicly.
- Personal insults.
- Unwelcome sexual attention.
- Excessive or unnecessary profanity.
- Repeated harassment. If someone asks you to stop, stop.
- Advocating for or encouraging any of the above.

## Security issues

Do not report a suspected vulnerability through any channel covered by this document. Follow
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).

## Reporting

Report a violation to <conduct@matterlabs.dev>, whether you are the subject of it or a witness to it, and report it
without delay. Reports reach the Matter Labs maintainers of this project and are handled confidentially. We do not share
a reporter's identity outside the group handling the report without their consent.

## Enforcement

Matter Labs maintains this project and decides on enforcement. Depending on severity, maintainers may issue a private
warning, remove content, or restrict or remove a participant's access to project spaces. Serious or repeated violations
can result in a permanent ban.
